How to play

Guess the secret word (the host chooses how long, from 4 to 20 letters). Each round uses 3 random symbols that always look very different from each other, and each one secretly means something different. You have to work out which is which.

Here's the twist: the symbols line up with the letters of the secret word, not with the letters you guessed. Symbol 1 is about the secret word's 1st letter, symbol 2 about its 2nd letter, and so on. Every letter of the word gets its own symbol.

Example: the secret word is SHAKE and the guess is THICK.

The 2nd symbol says the secret's H is in your guess in the right spot. The 4th says the secret's K is in your guess but in the wrong spot. The other three say S, A and E aren't in THICK at all.

Repeated letters: if you guess only one copy of a doubled letter and put it in the wrong spot, the first matching letter of the secret word gets the "wrong spot" symbol. If it's in the right spot, only that one gets "right spot" and you learn nothing about the other copy.

Playing in chat: type any real word that has the same number of letters as the secret word. If it's valid and nobody's guessed it yet this round, it goes straight onto the board with its symbols — no vote, no waiting. Anyone who types the secret word wins the round straight away, and there's no timer or limit on how many guesses the board can hold, so keep trying!

When the round ends, the tiles flip to show their colours, and the symbols' meanings are revealed.

Points: a fresh, valid guess earns a small point just for playing. Solving the round earns a bigger bonus — worth more for longer words and for solving quickly, before too many other guesses land on the board. Tap 🏆 any time to see the leaderboard.
